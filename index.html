Accountability engineering

Built to be honest.

Any system can be gamed. This one is designed like it knows. Every mechanism below exists because a kid tried to fake the simpler version.

🎙️ Speak-the-cue clips

Say the cue out loud before each rep. Nearly impossible to do mindlessly — it proves attention in a way silent film never can.

🔀 Rotating evidence asks

Side angle this week, game speed next, weak hand only after that. Unpredictability kills rehearsal.

🎯 Specificity prompts

"What broke down on rep 3?" — never "how did it go?" Generic questions get generic lies.

🌧️ Grace-day streaks

Two life-happens days a month; the streak pauses instead of resetting. Kids lie to unforgiving systems.

👀 Parents as witnesses

The Sunday summary makes the work visible. Ambient accountability — never policing, never nagging.

⚖️ The re-test as ground truth

Every six weeks, in front of a coach. You can't fake a left-hand finish. Everything else is signal; this is the audit.

The accountability engine

Ten mechanisms.
Zero guessing.

Accountability isn't a vibe — it's machinery. Here's every moving part, and the job each one does.

01 · Weekly deadlines

Rhythm is accountability. The week has a shape and every piece has a due time.

02 · Specific-evidence check-ins

Structured prompts demanding specifics. Never a lone "done" button.

03 · Spot-check clips

Random coach-requested clips with constraints. Keeps everyone honest without reviewing everything.

04 · Grace-day streaks

Honesty stays cheaper than faking, all season long.

05 · The game log

One minute after every game: did the focus survive contact?

06 · Sunday parent summary

Coach-approved, reads in a minute. The kid knows mom sees it.

07 · 6-week re-test

Ratings only move when it shows. The reckoning.

08 · Coach "needs you" list

Most important first. Nobody slips through silently.

09 · Pod streaks

Team accountability — never individual leaderboards. Those backfire.

10 · The guarantee, reversed

If we miss a feedback window, that week is free. Accountability goes both ways.
